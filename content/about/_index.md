I'm based in Groningen, the Netherlands, where I studied Artificial Intelligence at the University of Groningen. After
that I joined Slimmer AI (a startup working in the scientific domain), where I led the
recommenders team. We were acquired by Springer Nature in 2023, and I've been there since, now as Head of AI Engineering. If you want to get in touch, feel free to [reach out via email or social links](#get-in-touch).
